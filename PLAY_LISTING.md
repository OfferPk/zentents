## Full Description (Max 4000 characters)
Step into the tranquil woodland sanctuary of ZenTents, a peaceful and deeply satisfying offline Tents and Trees (Campsite) logic puzzle designed to gently engage your mind and soothe your spirit.

Across a peaceful forest grid, serene pine trees stand quietly awaiting their camping companions. Your objective is pure, elegant, and deductive: pitch cozy camping tents across the woodland clearing such that every tree has exactly one orthogonally adjacent tent, no two tents ever touch—not even diagonally—and each row and column matches its exact forest quota.

With no timers, no pressure, no ads, and zero network requirements, ZenTents offers a calm, mindful haven where classic Japanese grid logic meets the gentle quiet of nature.

### 🌲 KEY FEATURES

• 50 SOLVER-VERIFIED WOODLAND PUZZLES:
Embark on a mindful journey across 5 progressive forest packs:
- Pack 1: Pine Glade (Gentle 5x5 clearings to master tree and tent pairing)
- Pack 2: Birch Hollow (6x6 woodland maps with subtle corner constraints)
- Pack 3: Cedar Grove (7x7 forest trails with rich multi-tree interactions)
- Pack 4: Redwood Ridge (8x8 deep woods requiring careful boundary deduction)
- Pack 5: Whispering Wilds (9x9 expansive wilderness for master campsite planners)

• PROCEDURAL WOODLAND & CAMPSITE ACOUSTICS:
Immerse yourself in gentle, organic sound synthesis powered by the Web Audio API. Placing a tent produces a rich, tactile wooden peg strike; marking grass whispers like rustling leaves; completing a row quota rings with a soft woodland chime; and finishing a campsite evokes a resonant acoustic campfire cascade.

• 100% OFFLINE & PRIVACY-FIRST:
Play anywhere, anytime — deep in the mountains, during long flights, or curled up in bed. ZenTents requires no internet connection, no user accounts, and collects zero personal data.

• INTUITIVE CELL CYCLING & QUICK DRAG-MARKING:
Effortless controls tailored for touchscreens and desktop: tap to cycle through Empty → Grass Dot (ruled out) → Camping Tent → Empty. Effortlessly swipe across empty clearings to drag-paint grass markers.

• DEDUCTIVE HINT ASSISTANT & UNLIMITED UNDO:
Never feel stuck. An intelligent constraint-propagation solver analyzes your board in real-time, pointing out logical deductions such as satisfied quotas, isolated tree neighbors, or forced tent spots. Enjoy unlimited undo and instant board reset.

• DEEP EVERGREEN GLASSMORPHISM AESTHETICS:
A handcrafted visual experience blending deep forest moss, glowing campfire amber, high-DPI scaling, and crisp wooden textures.
